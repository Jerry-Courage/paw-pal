# Cellular Respiration
Cellular respiration releases usable energy from nutrients through linked biochemical reactions.
Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and produces carbon dioxide while transferring energy into ATP.
[PAGE_3_END]
[PAGE_4_START]
# Gas Exchange
Gas exchange means diffusion of oxygen and carbon dioxide across a respiratory surface.
A large surface area and a short diffusion distance increase the rate of gas exchange.
[PAGE_4_END]
[PAGE_5_START]
# Respiratory Structures
Some small animals exchange gases across a moist body surface.
Fish use gills in water, insects use branching tracheal systems, and mammals use lungs for air.
[PAGE_5_END]
[PAGE_6_START]
# Ventilation and Transport
Ventilation refreshes air at the exchange surface and maintains concentration gradients.
Blood transports oxygen from the lungs to tissues and returns carbon dioxide to the lungs.
